🐾 Pawse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

Pawse is a watch-together tool for two people. It is built to sync video playback between you and one partner. This policy explains, plainly, what it does and does not do with your data.

What Pawse handles

  • A display name you type — shown to your partner in the room. You choose it; it's optional.
  • Room codes — a short code that pairs you with your partner.
  • Playback events — play, pause, seek, and which video/timestamp you're on — relayed to your partner so you stay in sync.
  • For your own files — the movie file is streamed directly to your partner (peer-to-peer via WebRTC). It is never uploaded to us or stored on any server.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or any third party.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, or profiling.
  • We do not require an account, email, or password.
  • We do not store your playback history or files.

How data flows

Playback events pass through our relay server (pawse-relay.onrender.com) only to forward them to the other person in your room, in real time. They are not persisted. Rooms live in memory and disappear when empty. Your display name and last room code are stored locally in your own browser for convenience and never leave your device except to pair you with your partner.

Third-party content

The optional "Meow Feed" (cat memes shown while you wait) fetches images from a public meme API (meme-api.com, which relays Reddit content). No personal data is sent to it.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • storage — remember your name and room locally.
  • tabs — know which tab holds the video to sync.
  • Access to youtube.com — read and control the video player so it can sync.
  • Access to our relay — open the sync connection.
